R4. DELTA-PPL DECOMPOSITION — own pair minus STRANGER pair on the same items
==============================================================================
   own(i)      = NLL(own aligned) - NLL(own base)
   stranger(i) = NLL(other lineage aligned) - NLL(other lineage base)   [same item]

   pairs moves every stage equally, so STAGE DIFFERENCES in 'specific' are what
